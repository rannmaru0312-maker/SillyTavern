# Real product introductions — Instagram/Threads content policy

Approval context: 2026-11-08 JST. Owner approved adding **real, currently purchasable interior products** into the already-approved twice-weekly organic content. This is a **proposal and editorial guideline**, not permission for new spending, account changes, direct product advertising contracts, or additional posts.

## Editorial approach
- Start with **approximately two product-feature posts per calendar month**, replacing (not adding to) two of the existing Tuesday/Friday 19:00 Asia/Tokyo posts. Keep the remainder focused on practical interior design, space planning, lifestyle, cafés, and occasional other themes.
- Topics: lighting, storage, furniture, fabrics, small décor, and practical household items. Explain who the item may suit, how to coordinate a room, realistic limitations, dimensions/fit/safety considerations, and useful alternatives. Avoid promotional hype.
- For the current 2026-11-09–12-08 draft, a low-risk **proposal** is to adapt **Nov 20 (lighting)** and **Nov 27 (fabrics)** to include one verifiable real product apiece; November 10 and 13 are already reported as reserved and **must not be changed**. December slots remain provisional, with up to two product features if verified and within the existing cadence; defer if evidence, rights or quality are insufficient.
- Do not propose actual SKU, current price, stock, savings, specifications, endorsement, performance, or product photo until checked against reliable current official merchant/product information. Cite the official product listing URL, date checked and region (Japan) in the **internal review record**; avoid invented data or stale prices. Product links must be confirmed real, not guessed.
- Editorial selection should be independent and fit the character's interior coordinator persona. Do not claim the character personally bought, owns, visited, tested, or physically used the item.

## Visual and caption integrity
- Preserve the user's accepted selfie-style rule: Miya's identifiable face partially visible near the frame edge (roughly half face or slightly more as natural), while most of the image shows the useful room arrangement. Natural smartphone aesthetic, autumn/winter clothes, changing perspectives. No poster, infographic, overlaid product prices, logos, shopping call-outs, or typographic marketing designs unless specifically requested.
- The authentic appearance reference of Miya must be used if available. No substitute portrait.
- If a **specific product** is visually depicted, its product identity/design must be accurately represented and its image usage must be licensed/authorized. Do **not** label an approximate AI-generated generic lamp or room prop as a real SKU or imply it is a product photo.
- If authentic product photo rights or faithful depiction cannot be established, use an original **unbranded room illustration/photo-style scene** and discuss the verified product **only in the caption**, making clear that the image is a styling concept rather than an actual image of that product. Do not imply ownership or genuine experience.
- Keep captions natural Japanese with friendly Kansai dialect; practical tip first, optional real product detail, and a useful question. Avoid excessive hashtags; no hashtags containing AI. Make the appropriate Instagram AI-generation disclosure (independent of hashtag choices).

## Commercial disclosure, rights and financial controls
- Begin with independent editorial recommendations only; do not create affiliate accounts, referral arrangements, paid boosts, sponsorships, referral links, purchases, or paid APIs without explicit owner approval.
- If a future post has any advertiser control, compensation, gifts, material connection, or affiliate earning, verify the facts and comply with applicable transparent advertising disclosure and platform branded-content tools; clearly label advertising when appropriate (not merely an AI-generated flag). See the Japan Consumer Affairs Agency official stealth-marketing FAQ: https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/faq/stealth_marketing/ (checked 2026-11-08).
- Do not copy merchant photography, catalog images, influencer media, or logos without usage rights. Do not publish personal/private operational details.
- No guarantee of follower or sales growth.

## Workflow and measurement
1. Codex proposes candidate **categories**, possible product names only with links and dated sourcing, useful context, factual limitations, and captions; it does not schedule/publish or claim access to Metricool.
2. The authorized publishing assistant checks both Instagram and Threads account identities, verifies current public merchant product information, rights and compliance, checks duplicate reservations, checks approved reference image and caption, then schedules one cross-post only if all conditions pass.
3. Compare product-feature posts against non-product posts **descriptively** using permitted aggregate metrics (reach, saves, shares, profile visits, follower change when attributable). Mark missing data unavailable; do not claim causation from tiny samples.
4. Existing approved cadence remains Tuesday/Friday 19:00 JST with Monday/Thursday production. No previously published or scheduled post is altered without specific approval; no new apps, spending, or product promotions of the unreleased app.
